DOCSIS 3.1 OFDM/OFDMA Estimator

Downstream & upstream throughput estimator based on DOCSIS 3.1 PHY specification parameters

Channel Parameters
Channel Width (MHz) ? Total OFDM channel bandwidth. DOCSIS 3.1 supports 24–192 MHz downstream channels in multiples of the subcarrier spacing. Max is 192 MHz per CM-SP-PHYv3.1.
Must be 24–192 MHz
Subcarrier Spacing ? 50 kHz → 4096-point FFT at 204.8 MHz sample rate (20 µs symbol). 25 kHz → 8192-point FFT (40 µs symbol). 50 kHz is most commonly deployed. [DOCSIS 3.1 §7.4.2]
Modulation Order ? Average modulation per data subcarrier. DOCSIS 3.1 supports QPSK through 4096-QAM. Higher orders require better SNR. [CM-SP-PHYv3.1 §7.6]
Guard Band (MHz) ? Total guard band removed from the occupied spectrum (both edges combined). Prevents interference with adjacent channels. Typical: 1 MHz. Range: 0–4 MHz.
Must be 0–4 MHz
Excluded Spectrum (MHz) ? Spectrum notched out within the channel (e.g., for legacy analog or narrowband interference). Reduces usable subcarriers. Range: 0–20 MHz.
Must be 0–20 MHz
Cyclic Prefix (samples) ? CP duration in samples at 204.8 MHz sample rate. 512 samples = 2.5 µs, sufficient for most HFC plant delay spreads. Larger CP wastes spectral efficiency but improves multipath tolerance. [CM-SP-PHYv3.1 §7.4.4]

Channel Parameters
Channel Width (MHz) ? Total OFDMA upstream channel width. DOCSIS 3.1 supports 6.4–96 MHz for low-split, up to 684 MHz for extended high-split. Common deployments: 48 or 96 MHz. [CM-SP-PHYv3.1 §8.1]
Must be 6.4–684 MHz
Subcarrier Spacing ? OFDMA upstream supports 6.25, 25, or 50 kHz subcarrier spacing. 25 kHz is most common. 6.25 kHz is used for extended reach. [CM-SP-PHYv3.1 §8.4.2]
Modulation Order ? Upstream modulation order per data subcarrier. DOCSIS 3.1 supports QPSK through 4096-QAM upstream. 1024-QAM is a practical limit for many plant conditions.
Minislot Size (subcarriers) ? Number of subcarriers per minislot. For 50 kHz spacing: 8 subcarriers/minislot. For 25 kHz spacing: 16 subcarriers/minislot. Minislots are the fundamental upstream allocation unit. [CM-SP-PHYv3.1 §8.5]
Guard Band (MHz) ? Guard band at channel edges (both sides combined). Prevents interference with adjacent upstream bands. Typical: 0.4–1 MHz total.
Must be 0–4 MHz
Excluded Spectrum (MHz) ? Spectrum notched out within the upstream channel (e.g., for ingress avoidance or narrowband RFI). Range: 0–10 MHz.
Must be 0–10 MHz
